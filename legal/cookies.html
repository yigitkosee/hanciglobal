What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files saved in your browser when you visit a website. In this policy, "cookies" also covers similar technologies used for the same purposes, such as browser storage (localStorage). For general information on the personal data processed, see our Privacy Policy. Data controller: Assetto Gayrimenkul Yatırım A.Ş..

How to control cookies

Essential cookies are needed for the website to work. Analytics and advertising cookies are not essential; you can block or delete them in your browser settings (the "Privacy" or "Cookies" section in Chrome, Safari, Firefox and Edge). To opt out of Google Analytics on all websites, use Google’s browser add-on; to turn off personalised ads, use Google Ad Settings. Blocking cookies may affect how parts of the website work.
